## Interpretation
- Cluster structure is **weak**: cosine silhouette 0.014–0.022 for every k, and agreement with the reference categories is near zero (ARI ≤ 0.027, NMI ≤ 0.070). This is consistent with H3: some themes are coherent, but correspondence with operational labels is poor.
- A few themes are recognisable: login and password terms (k=9 #6, #3 → AUTH-dominant), generic "التطبيق لا يعمل" (k=9 #1, #4 → AVAIL-dominant), and post-update problems ("التحديث الجديد / بعد التحديث", k=9 #7 → OTHER/AVAIL/UX). Most clusters are driven by generic or stylistic words (برنامج، جدا، للاسف، فاشل) rather than failure types.
- Conclusion: unsupervised K-Means on short reviews is **not** a substitute for the supervised classifier. At most it helps browse recurring wording. No alerting is built on it.
